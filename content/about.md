# About AlexJ.blog

Welcome to my personal corner of the internet. I'm Alex, and this blog is where I share essays and observations about life, creativity, and the small moments that often go unnoticed.

I started this blog as a place to explore thoughts that deserve more space than a social media post but don't quite fit anywhere else. It's a space for reflection, curiosity, and occasional insight.

## What I Write About

My writing tends to wander through various interests and experiences:

- **Living Spaces**: DIY projects around the house and creating environments that feel like home
- **Creating**: Baking adventures, coding projects, writing experiments, and photography
- **Attention & Balance**: Navigating life with ADHD, finding focus, and managing burnout
- **Digital vs. Physical**: The tension between digital convenience and meaningful engagement
- **Exploration**: Travel experiences and discovering new perspectives
- **Reading & Learning**: Books that change how I think and what I've learned from them
